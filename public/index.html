Know the Warning Signs

Get to know what is normal for you, so you notice when something changes.

🔘

New lump

A new lump or swelling in the breast or underarm.

🩹

Skin changes

Dimpling, puckering, thickening or an unusual texture.

↔️

Shape or size changes

A new change in the size or shape of one breast.

🎯

Nipple changes

A new change in nipple position or appearance, such as new inversion.

💧

Unusual discharge

Nipple discharge, especially if bloody or spontaneous.

⏳

Persistent changes

Redness, swelling or any change that is unusual for you and does not go away.

Notice something unusual? Don't panic — but don't ignore it. Talk to a healthcare professional.

What is breast cancer screening?

Screening aims to detect breast cancer before symptoms become noticeable. The appropriate screening method and frequency depend on factors such as age and individual risk. Screening recommendations may vary between guidelines and countries, so discuss your personal screening needs with a healthcare professional.

Know the difference

ScreeningDiagnostic evaluation
For people who may have no symptomsFor people with concerning symptoms or findings
Looks for cancer before symptoms appearInvestigates an abnormal finding
May involve mammography and other tests depending on age and riskMay involve clinical examination, imaging, biopsy, etc.

The BreastAware assessment is not a screening test. It promotes screening awareness and early detection — actual screening is performed through appropriate healthcare services.
